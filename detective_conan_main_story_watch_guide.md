# Detective Conan — Main-Story Watch Guide

**Scope:** Original Japanese TV episode numbering.  
**Goal:** Follow the overarching Detective Conan narrative while skipping ordinary standalone cases and anime-original filler.  
**Coverage:** Episode 1 through Episode 1212 (current through September 2026).  
**Curated main-story cut:** **259 TV episodes** in the table below.  

> This is intentionally stricter than a manga-canon list. Manga-canon does **not** mean main-story relevant: hundreds of canon murder cases are essentially self-contained. This guide keeps Black Organization/APTX material, Haibara, FBI/CIA/Akai, Vermouth/Kir/Bourbon/Rum, major Shinichi–Ran identity/relationship developments, and prerequisite cases needed to understand later reveals.

---

| **This guide** | **259 episodes through Ep. 1212** | XerBlade strict backbone + later canon developments verified against Detective Conan World |

The number is therefore very close to the “roughly 300 episodes” version people often refer to. It is not an official list; it is a deliberately compressed viewing route.

---

## 2. Important numbering warning

This guide uses the **original Japanese episode numbers** used by Detective Conan World and most long-running fan references.

Some international services renumber long TV specials as multiple episodes. Consequently, a Japanese episode such as **345** may correspond to several international episode slots. If your streaming service's number does not match, search by **episode title**.

IMDb has the same complication: it organizes Detective Conan using its own season/episode structure, which does not always map cleanly to the original Japanese numbering. For that reason, the IMDb column below contains a rating only where the mapping was directly verified. `—` means “not safely mapped/verified,” not “unrated.” IMDb ratings also change over time.

Verified examples at the time this guide was prepared:

- Japanese Episode 1, **Roller Coaster Murder Case** — **8.1/10** on IMDb.
- Japanese Episode 345, **Head-to-Head Match with the Black Organization: A Dual Mystery on a Full Moon Night** — **9.6/10** on IMDb and currently among the series' highest-rated episodes.

### Why the early anime needs special handling

The first years occasionally altered manga material that later proved important. In particular, the manga's Shinkansen case contains Gin/Vodka information that the anime replaced with one-off criminals, and the Akemi case was reworked before Episode 128 restored the plotline. This is why a simple “watch every manga-adapted episode” filter is not ideal.

# 7. Movies: required or optional?

For the TV/manga canon storyline, the movies are generally **optional**. They often exploit current character identities and relationships, so they can spoil the series if watched too early, but historically they do not determine the main TV plot.

For a Black Organization-focused side watch, the most directly relevant movies are:

| Movie | Title | Relevance |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 | *Countdown to Heaven* | Black Organization involvement |
| 13 | *The Raven Chaser* | Heavy BO focus |
| 20 | *The Darkest Nightmare* | BO + intelligence agencies |
| 26 | *Black Iron Submarine* | Strong BO/Haibara focus |

Other films contain Amuro/Akai/Kid material but are not necessary for following the manga-derived TV story.

# 8. What this guide deliberately excludes

It excludes, unless required by the main plot:

- anime-original filler;
- standalone manga murder cases;
- most Detective Boys-only adventures;
- most Kaito Kid cases;
- most Metropolitan Police romance cases;
- most Heiji/Kazuha romance progression;
- most Sonoko/Makoto material;
- recurring-character development that has little effect on the Black Organization/APTX/Shinichi narrative.

This is why the guide is much shorter than a standard “canon episodes” list.

If you care strongly about Heiji/Kazuha, Kaito Kid, Sato/Takagi, or other secondary arcs, use XerBlade's broader important-episode guide rather than this strict route.

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

# 11. Practical recommendation

For a first serious watch, follow the **259-episode list in order** and do not read plot summaries before watching. When you reach a case that feels slow, avoid skipping midway: Conan frequently places the metanarrative clue at the end of an otherwise ordinary murder case.

For a rewatch, the strict BO appearance list is viable because you already know the prerequisite identities and relationships.
