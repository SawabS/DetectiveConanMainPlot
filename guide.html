Sources and ratings

Detective Conan World supplies Japanese titles, original broadcasts, and case references. XerBlade provides a reference for central-story cases and prerequisites. This checklist contains 255 distinct episodes across nine arcs.

Ratings and votes come from IMDb's non-commercial datasets: episode IDs, titles, and ratings. IMDb uses different season numbering. Mappings were checked by title, year, and multipart order, including translated title variants; reruns were excluded. IMDb scores are weighted user ratings and change over time.

Episode data records each IMDb ID, IMDb title and numbering, mapping method, vote count, check date, runtime, duration source, and mapping audit trail. N/A, if present after a refresh, means no rating was returned for that mapped entry. See CHANGELOG for corrections.
